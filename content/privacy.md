# HunBAN Privacy Notice

Version 2.0, effective 5 November 2026.

## 1. Introduction and scope

The Hungarian Business Angel Network Association ("HunBAN", "we") processes personal data to run an angel investor association and its wider community of investors, founders, mentors and ecosystem partners. This notice tells you what we collect, why, on what legal basis, who sees it, how long we keep it and what your rights are under the General Data Protection Regulation ((EU) 2016/679, "GDPR") and Hungarian Act CXII of 2011 on Informational Self-Determination and Freedom of Information ("Infotv.").

It covers our website, our membership and startup application forms, our CRM, our events (including Startup Arena and other event formats we are running on our own), our newsletter and every other HunBAN programme. Third-party sites we link to (for example Luma or LinkedIn) have their own privacy policies.

HunBAN itself does not invest. Our members make their own, independent investment decisions. We facilitate introductions and charge founders no fees at any stage.

## 6. How we handle your startup application

Access to your application widens step by step, and only as you move forward in the process.

1. **Submission.** You apply through our online form. Your answers and files go straight into our CRM. Only HunBAN staff can see them at this stage.
2. **Completeness and fit check.** The HunBAN team checks that the application is complete and fits our criteria (for example a connection to Hungary and an angel-sized round). Where we need specialist input, we may consult an individual expert from our network, for example on a technology, market or regulatory question. We share only what the question needs, and ask the expert to keep it confidential. Every complete application gets a response.
3. **Evaluation call.** If you pass, we invite you to a short call. We may record and transcribe it with Fathom so our notes are accurate; we tell you at the start, and we don't record if you ask us not to. Our notes, and any recording and transcript, are stored with your application.
4. **Investor review.** Shortlisted startups are reviewed by HunBAN's board, who are themselves angel investors. We may also show your profile to individual members whose stated interests match yours.
5. **Exposure to the membership.** If you are invited to pitch at Startup Arena, a Founder Q&A session or our internal deal flow platform, your profile and deck become available to all HunBAN members and non-member investors in the network. Startup Arena is a public event and pitches may be recorded or streamed (Section 12).
6. **Introductions.** We make warm introductions to specific members, mentors or partners. Anyone outside the HunBAN membership gets your materials only with your prior agreement.
7. **Parked or declined.** We keep your file for the period in Section 10 so we can reconnect when your stage fits better. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

No step is decided automatically by software. People decide at every stage.

## 7. How we handle startup materials

HunBAN and its members handle what you submit in your startup's interest. Forwarding decks and making introductions is how angel investing works, so we don't promise strict confidentiality. We promise good faith.

**Who sees your materials**

| Who | When | Bound by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HunBAN staff | From submission | Their employment or service contract |
| HunBAN board | From the investor review step | The HunBAN Member Code of Conduct |
| HunBAN members | From the investor review step, or when you accept an invitation to pitch | The HunBAN Member Code of Conduct |
| Experts we consult during evaluation | Only with your knowledge | Asked to keep it confidential |
| Investors and partners outside HunBAN | Only with your agreement | Their own terms |

**What members commit to.** Under the [HunBAN Member Code of Conduct](/code-of-conduct), members may use and pass on your materials to evaluate an investment, bring in co-investors or connect you with people who can help. When they do, they put your startup's interests first, act in good faith and stop if you ask them to. They never pass on information received under a separately signed NDA, and never use your materials to help a competing business.

**What we don't do.** We don't sell or publish your materials, and we don't use them for anything other than the purposes in this notice.

**No NDAs at the application stage.** In line with international angel and venture practice, neither HunBAN nor its members sign NDAs to review an application or a pitch. Keep trade secrets out of your application, such as source code, detailed technical architecture, unfiled patent details or customer contract terms. Share those later, in due diligence, directly with a specific investor and under an NDA if needed.

**Public pitches are public.** Anything you present on stage at Startup Arena or another public event, and anything recorded there, is not confidential.

**If something goes wrong.** Report suspected misuse of your information to info@hunban.eu. We handle it under the Member Code of Conduct, which can lead to a member's expulsion.

## 9. Transfers outside the EU/EEA

We keep data in the EU/EEA where we can, but some of our service providers are based in the USA, and our community includes Hungarian investors living abroad.

- **US service providers.** Where a provider is on the Data Privacy Framework list for the data concerned, we rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision (EU) 2023/1795 of 10 July 2023. Where a provider is not certified, we use the Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Decision (EU) 2021/914). Where we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses, we assess whether they give effective protection in the circumstances, add safeguards where needed, and suspend transfers where adequate protection cannot be ensured.
- **Countries with an adequacy decision** (for example the United Kingdom or Switzerland). Transfers need no extra safeguard.
- **Investors in other countries.** We share a startup's materials with an investor outside the EU/EEA only with the explicit consent of each person whose data is in them, after telling them which country is involved and the possible risks of a transfer without an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards (GDPR Art. 49(1)(a)). Founders can remove team members' personal data from materials before they are shared .

You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards that apply to a transfer.

## 11. Where data comes from, automated decisions and AI tools

**Sources.** Most data comes directly from you. We also receive or look up data from:

- founders, about the team members named in their materials (Group D)
- members, partners, accelerators and other founders who refer a startup or an investor to us
- public sources, such as LinkedIn, the Hungarian company register (e-cegjegyzek.hu), company websites and press coverage, to verify information and improve matchmaking
- co-organisers of joint events, who share registration lists with us

**People named in startup materials.** We usually don't collect your data from you directly (GDPR Art. 14). Founders confirm on the application form that they have told the people named in their materials about this notice. When we contact you, or share materials containing your data beyond the HunBAN team, we inform you directly, at the latest within one month (Art. 14(3)). You have the same rights as everyone else, including the right to object.

**No automated decisions.** We make no decisions with legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing, including profiling (GDPR Art. 22). Matchmaking suggestions are made and checked by people.

**AI-assisted tools.** We use Claude Pro by Anthropic to help summarise applications, sort email and draft replies, and Fathom to record, transcribe and summarise calls (Section 6). A HunBAN team member reviews every output and takes every decision. We keep Claude's model training setting switched off, so our chats are not used to train Anthropic's models.

## 12. Photos, recordings and streaming at events

HunBAN events, including Startup Arena, may be photographed, filmed, recorded or streamed. We use this content on hunban.eu, LinkedIn, Facebook and in our newsletter, share recordings of pitches with investors, and include it in reports to the funders of our events.

- **Crowd shots and general event footage.** Under Section 2:48(2) of the Hungarian Civil Code, crowd recordings and recordings of public participation in public affairs need no individual consent. We rely on legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) and announce recording on the invitation and at the venue.
- **Pitching founders, speakers and panellists.** Recordings that focus on you individually need your consent (Ptk. 2:48(1), GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). We ask for it when we invite you to pitch or speak, and you can say no or withdraw later.
- **Opting out.** Tell the host or photographer at the event and we will keep you out of individual shots and remove you from published material on request.

Removing content already shared by third parties on their own channels may be outside our control, but we will ask them to do so.

## 13. Website, cookies and newsletter

**Website.** hunban.eu sets no cookies, so there is no cookie banner. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views: it works without cookies, identifies visits only by a temporary hash that is discarded after 24 hours, and gives us aggregated statistics, not individual profiles. Our hosting provider, Vercel, keeps basic technical server logs for security.

**Public profiles and testimonials.** hunban.eu shows members, founders we support and partners, with name, photo, role, organisation and LinkedIn link, and quotes from founders and investors. We publish these only with the person's consent, and remove them on request to info@hunban.eu.

**Embedded forms and links.** Our startup application form is hosted by ClickUp, and event registration by Luma. When you open them through our links, these services may set their own cookies under their own policies. The ecosystem map on hunban.eu loads its background map from OpenFreeMap, which receives your IP address and basic browser data when the map loads, so it can deliver the map tiles. OpenFreeMap sets no cookies.

**Newsletter.** We send it only to people who signed up. Every issue has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing never affects your membership or application. We measure opens and clicks to improve content; you can block this by disabling images in your email client.

## 14. Data security and breaches

We protect personal data with technical and organisational measures that fit a small association handling confidential deal flow:

- access limited to the people who need it, with startup files restricted to staff until the founder agrees to wider sharing
- two-factor authentication on all HunBAN accounts
- encrypted connections (HTTPS) and providers with recognised security certifications
- written data processing agreements with every service provider
- a record of processing activities (GDPR Art. 30)

If a personal data breach occurs, we report it to the Hungarian data protection authority (NAIH) without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, unless it is unlikely to put anyone's rights at risk (Art. 33), and inform you without delay if it is likely to put your rights at high risk (Art. 34).

Email info@hunban.eu to use any of these rights. It is free. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we reply without undue delay and within one month. For complex or numerous requests we may extend this by two months, and tell you why within the first month (GDPR Art. 12(3)).

**Complaints.** You can complain to the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH): 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9-11; postal address 1363 Budapest, Pf. 9; phone +36 1 391 1400; ugyfelszolgalat@naih.hu; www.naih.hu.
